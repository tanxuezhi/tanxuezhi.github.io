01 / Biography

Connecting atmospheric processes to water and society.

Prof. Tan is a Professor and PhD Supervisor at the School of Civil Engineering, Sun Yat-sen University. His work advances understanding of hydroclimate variability, extreme weather, climate-change impacts and sustainable agro-hydrological systems.

He received PhD training in Water Resources Engineering at the University of Alberta and in Hydraulics & Hydropower Engineering at Wuhan University, following undergraduate study in Hydrology and Water Resources at Northwest A&F University.

02 / Research interests

Five connected research directions.

01

Extreme precipitation

Large-scale connected events, moisture transport, circulation dynamics, attribution and projections.

02

Drought–flood hazards

Flash drought, precipitation whiplash, drought termination and compounding risks.

03

Water resources evolution

Runoff attribution, water budgets, vegetation–climate feedbacks and water security.

04

Agricultural resilience

Water–nitrogen cycling, irrigation, crop productivity and climate-risk prevention.

05

Renewable energy

Climate-sensitive wind, solar and hydropower resources; complementarity and grid resilience.

Research approach

From physical diagnosis to adaptation decisions.

  1. 01 Observe Stations · satellites · reanalysis · field experiments
  2. 02 Explain Moisture pathways · circulation · land–atmosphere feedbacks
  3. 03 Attribute Large ensembles · CMIP · statistical and process models
  4. 04 Project & decide AI downscaling · risk maps · resilient management
